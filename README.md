# StationOS

**Your business. Your customers. Your radio station.**

StationOS is an online radio production and broadcasting system for businesses, independent radio makers and communities. Build a station around your music, your audience and your editorial direction, with fictional AI presenters and an AI production assistant to help run it.

From a shop’s daytime soundtrack to a specialist music station, give your audience something of its own to tune into.

[Explore StationOS](https://stationos.cloud) · [Get started](docs/getting-started.md) · [Read the guides](docs/README.md)

## More than a playlist

- **Shape your sound.** Connect a compatible music server and build playlists from genres, albums, artists, composers, dates and individual recordings.
- **Give it a voice.** Create fictional DJs with their own personalities, voices and editorial direction. They select eligible music and prepare spoken links.
- **Plan your week.** Schedule recurring shows with different presenters and playlists. A default playlist covers the gaps.
- **Put AI to work.** Ask the production assistant to help create and manage stations, music selections, DJs and schedules.
- **Programme beyond music.** Keep podcast episodes and audiobook chapters in sequence, and make room for promotions, sponsor messages and advertising.
- **Bring listeners together.** Share a station page or an internet-radio stream. Everyone joins the same programme.
- **Invite participation.** Enable requests and shoutouts for selected shows, with review before contributions reach the programme.

Run several stations from shared music sources and DJ profiles. Each station keeps its own identity, playlists, schedule and stream address.

## You set the direction

Choose the eligible music, presenter personalities and programme brief. StationOS handles music selection and generated presentation within those boundaries. Review upcoming selections, adjust the mix and inspect what was broadcast from the producer workspace.

Production starts when the first listener tunes in and stops when the last listener leaves. Opening a station page does not start playback. When AI preparation is unavailable, music can continue from the eligible pool.

## Choose how to run it

**Self-hosted:** install the open-source software on your own infrastructure. You provide a compatible music source, configure any AI services, and manage updates and backups. Start with the [installation guide](docs/getting-started.md).

**Hosted:** have the platform maintained for you while you manage your music and programming.

**Fully managed:** agree your station’s sound and requirements, with music sourcing and station operation included in the service scope.

Visit [StationOS](https://stationos.cloud/#options) to explore the service options. Commercial scope and pricing are agreed separately.

## Before you start

Self-hosting needs Docker Compose, a reachable UPnP music server and someone comfortable maintaining a server. Audio stays on your music server; StationOS imports its catalogue. AI presentation requires configured text and speech services, which may charge for use. Music-only listening can run without AI configuration.

The software is free to use under [GPL-3.0-only](LICENSE). Hosting, AI services and music permissions are separate from the software licence. See the [practical questions](docs/faq.md) for costs, listening behaviour and current boundaries.
