# StationOS guides

Create a station with its own sound, presenters and programme, then give your audience a simple way to listen.

## Start here

1. [Get started](getting-started.md) — install StationOS, connect music and hear your first broadcast.
2. [Run your station](running-your-station.md) — choose music, create DJs, schedule shows and manage participation.
3. [Listening and sharing](listening.md) — publish a listener page and connect compatible players.

## Set up and maintain

- [AI setup](ai-setup.md) — connect services and choose models and voices.
- [Hosting and maintenance](hosting.md) — networking, public access, backups, updates and troubleshooting.
- [Practical questions](faq.md) — costs, requirements and listening behaviour.

For Hosted and Fully managed options, visit [StationOS](https://stationos.cloud/#options).
