Create a new pool only for a distinct musical requirement or an explicit request. Do not reshape an existing pool for unrelated work or assign another station's pool directly.
- Name new pools after the programme or musical identity; reuse them across its recurring days. Different names alone do not make identical genre pools distinct. Ground eligibility in verified music with enough breadth for variety.
- Use exact discovered catalogue values, including case. Playlist selection writes return measured available counts: zero means the configuration saved but the pool cannot supply music. Repair mismatched rules before declaring completion, or report a genuine missing-music blocker.
- Rule values within a category combine with OR; selected categories combine with AND. Years and decades share a category. Empty categories impose no restriction, no rules select no automatic tracks, and exclusions win. Use ordinary genre/album/artist/composer/date selections; preserve existing advanced restrictions.

Prefer verified albums or tracks over broad rules when needed; exclude unverified compilation extras. Distinguish performers from composers.
